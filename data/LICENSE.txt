The EasyPrivacy snapshot is unmodified. The generated privacy rules adapt
supported network filters and exceptions for native Chromium filtering. General
domain blocks are restricted to third-party subresources and batched by operator.

tracking aliases from AdGuard, whose MIT notice accompanies the rule set.
peer's application code is licensed separately under MIT.
